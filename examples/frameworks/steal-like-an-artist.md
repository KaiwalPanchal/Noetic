# Framework: Steal Like an Artist

**Source:** Austin Kleon, *Steal Like an Artist: 10 Things Nobody Told You About Being Creative* (2012)
**Linked Interests:** [[interests#Content curation & taste]], [[interests#Site & design replication]]
**Tags:** #framework #creativity #curation

---

## Core Idea
Nothing is original. Creative work is the **curation and transformation of influences**. Your taste is what you choose to collect, and your style is what happens when you remix many sources until they become yours.

## Principles
1. **Steal like an artist.** Collect good ideas; you are a mashup of what you let into your life. Build a genealogy of influences.
2. **Don't wait until you know who you are to get started.** Identity comes from doing; "fake it till you make it" means imitating forward.
3. **Write the book you want to read.** Make the thing you wish existed.
4. **Use your hands.** Step away from the screen; the body thinks too.
5. **Side projects and hobbies are important.** Productive procrastination and cross-pollination are where novelty lives.
6. **The secret: do good work and share it with people.** Quality plus distribution.
7. **Geography is no longer our master.** Build your scene online.
8. **Be nice (the world is a small town).** Credit generously; hang out with people better than you.
9. **Be boring (it's the only way to get work done).** Routine protects creative energy.
10. **Creativity is subtraction.** Constraints and deletion create style.

## Mental Moves / Procedures
1. **Swipe file:** when something stops you, save it *with a note on why*. The "why" is your taste, written down.
2. **Climb the family tree:** for any work you admire, find 3 of its influences, then their influences.
3. **Steal from many:** never take from a single source. Collect at least 3–5 references before making anything.
4. **Copy the mechanism, transform the expression:** reverse-engineer *how* it works; change *what it looks and sounds like*.
5. **Emulate, don't imitate:** imitation copies the surface; emulation copies the thinking, and your failures to match it become your style.
6. **Credit out loud:** name every influence publicly.
7. **Subtract:** before shipping, remove one element and see whether it got better.

## When to Apply
- **Content:** curate before you create. Every post traces an idea's genealogy and credits its sources; build in public instead of waiting.
- **Code:** before building UI, collect 3–5 reference sites (awwwards, dribbble, portfolios). Break each one into elements, steal mechanisms (layout systems, motion patterns), transform the expression (type, color, copy), and credit the references. See `/apply steal-like-an-artist code <urls>`.
- **Projects / Decisions:** when stuck on "what should this be," find who already solved an adjacent problem in a different domain and remix it.

## Anti-Patterns
- Copying one source wholesale (plagiarism, not research).
- Collecting forever without making (a swipe file with no output is hoarding).
- Copying the surface (colors, copy, assets) instead of the mechanism.
- Hiding influences to look original.
