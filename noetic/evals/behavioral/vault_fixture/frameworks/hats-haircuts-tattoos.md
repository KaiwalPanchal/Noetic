## 1. Core Idea
Categorize choices by reversibility: Hats (zero friction), Haircuts (moderate time), Tattoos (permanent scars).

## 2. Core Heuristic & Trigger
- **When in evaluating engineering decisions and RFC proposals** → Prioritize **Rigorous deliberation on Tattoos** over _Uniform committee review across all tasks_ because trivial decisions squander organizational momentum while unexamined tattoos inflict multi-year lock-in. (Rule: `Classify every RFC proposal as Hat, Haircut, or Tattoo before debating implementation`) [Tier 2] [Tags: governance, decision-making] [Anchor: Engineers frequently make two equal and opposite mistakes: they agonize for weeks over trivia that could be reversed in ten seconds, and they carelessly greenlight architectural decisions over lunch that will trap the company for the next decade.]
- **When in reviewing changes categorized as Hats** → Prioritize **Immediate autonomous execution** over _Protracted team deliberation_ because the cost of consensus on reversible decisions exceeds the cost of making and correcting an error. (Rule: `Approve Hats instantly without committee meetings; reverse immediately if suboptimal`) [Tier 2] [Tags: governance, execution-speed] [Anchor: Deliberation Rule: Zero committee review. Implement immediately; reverse immediately if suboptimal.]
- **The Core Question / Razor:** "How much pain and calendar time does it take to undo this if we are 100% wrong?"
- **Governing Invariant:** Deliberation time must scale monotonically with decision reversibility cost.

## 3. Mental Moves / Procedures
1. Assess the rollback friction and calendar duration.
2. Assign category: Hat, Haircut, or Tattoo.
3. Enforce deliberation budget: 0h for Hats, <48h for Haircuts, formal ADR for Tattoos.

## 4. Where to Apply
- **Primary Domain:** decision-governance
### Ontology Primitives
- **Hat** (`state`): A lightweight decision reversible in minutes with zero blast radius.
- **Haircut** (`state`): A decision reversible in days or weeks with moderate switching effort.
- **Tattoo** (`state`): A permanent or multi-year commitment with extreme switching friction or irreversible data mutation.

### Trade-Off Matrix
| Tension Dimension | Recommended Choice | Sacrificed | Gained | Boundary Flip Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Deliberation Depth | Extreme scrutiny on Tattoos, near-zero scrutiny on Hats | The illusion of uniform democratic consensus on all issues | Rapid execution velocity paired with foolproof protection against catastrophic lock-in | When a nominally reversible Hat creates legal liability or cryptographic key compromise |

## 5. Anti-Patterns & Boundaries (When it Breaks)
- **Tattoo Disguised as Hat**: Treating a public wire protocol or database engine change as a casual quick PR → System locks into an unmaintainable external contract with no migration path (Recovery: Halt PR immediately and mandate formal Mode B sparring and ADR review)
