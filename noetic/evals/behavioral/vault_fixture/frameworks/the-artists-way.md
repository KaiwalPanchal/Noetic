## 1. Core Idea
Creativity is an innate, universal spiritual energy inherent to all humans rather than a rare elite talent. Blockages are psychological defense mechanisms rooted in fear, judgment, and internalized criticism. By committing to sequential emotional rehabilitation and disciplined, uncritical practices—such as daily longhand stream-of-consciousness writing and trusting alignment with a higher creative force—individuals dismantle the inner Censor, heal the inner artist child, and restore prolific creative abundance.

## 2. Core Heuristic & Trigger
