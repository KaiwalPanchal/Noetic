## 1. Core Idea
High-agency living requires prioritizing physiological regulation over cognitive rationalization, dignified vulnerability over defensive retaliation, and deterministic execution over speculative perfectionism.

## 2. Core Heuristic & Trigger
- **When in Navigating an escalating public argument or conflict** → Prioritize **Walking away in silence** over _Defending your reputation or proving you are right_ because Pride-driven disputes waste energy and create enemies for zero ROI unless actual money or an essential relationship is directly on the line. (Rule: `Walking away in silence`) [Tier 1]
- **When in Feeling acute anxiety, panic, or urge to react in a personal or professional relationship** → Prioritize **Buying 90 seconds and treating emotions with robust skepticism** over _Immediately analyzing or acting on the urgent feeling_ because An impression is not a prognosis, fear is not a fact, and feelings above a 5/10 are old emotional tracks projecting onto the present. (Rule: `Buying 90 seconds and treating emotions with robust skepticism`) [Tier 1]
- **When in Setting boundaries with a draining or volatile collaborator or friend** → Prioritize **Making a calm, dignified avowal of limited bandwidth and vulnerable state** over _Dramatic severance, icy isolation, or tactical ghosting_ because Extreme detachment escalates drama while clear, non-negotiable structural schedules preserve integrity. (Rule: `Making a calm, dignified avowal of limited bandwidth and vulnerable state`) [Tier 1]
- **When in Communicating difficult feedback, hurt, or unmet expectations** → Prioritize **Translating anger through the Honest, Kind, and Polite filter by naming vulnerable fears and needs directly** over _Explosive confrontation or silent sulking_ because Sulking is a game expecting mind-reading, and anger triggers immediate defensive walls. (Rule: `Translating anger through the Honest, Kind, and Polite filter by naming vulnerable fears and needs directly`) [Tier 1]
- **When in Facing high uncertainty, dread, or intimidation in starting a project or difficult conversation** → Prioritize **Doing it scared with small, ugly, disposable actions** over _Waiting for emotional regulation, confidence, or comprehensive mastery_ because Emotions are physiological signals that follow action rather than precede it. (Rule: `Doing it scared with small, ugly, disposable actions`) [Tier 1]
- **When in Evaluating relationships or long-term collaborators** → Prioritize **The good enough framework with calibrated, progressive disclosure** over _Demanding perfect soulmate alignment or zero-friction compatibility_ because Compatibility is the hard-won ongoing achievement of adulthood rather than a magical starting condition. (Rule: `The good enough framework with calibrated, progressive disclosure`) [Tier 1]
- **When in Designing AI workflows or knowledge tools** → Prioritize **Deterministic structural contracts and tiny tuned lenses** over _Giant catch-all mega-prompts and autonomous multi-agent swarms_ because Probabilistic loops without hard schema gates compound errors and produce unfocused hype-slop. (Rule: `Deterministic structural contracts and tiny tuned lenses`) [Tier 1]
- **When in A close relationship is in silent tension or distance** → Prioritize **Initiating a low-stakes reassuring check-in** over _Waiting for the other person to notice and pursue you_ because People rarely chase silence and silence gets filled with invented catastrophe. (Rule: `Initiating a low-stakes reassuring check-in`) [Tier 1]
