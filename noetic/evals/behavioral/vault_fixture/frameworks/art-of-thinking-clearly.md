## 1. Core Idea
Human reasoning deviates from logic and probability in predictable, systematic directions rather than through random mistakes. Because intuitive mental shortcuts are fundamentally mismatched with complex modern environments, clear thinking is achieved not by striving for unattainable cognitive perfection, but through the rigorous negative art of cataloging, spotting, and systematically eliminating recurring cognitive traps and mental fallacies.

## 2. Core Heuristic & Trigger
- **When in Making high-stakes financial, business, or personal decisions where intuitive judgments are vulnerable to well-documented psychological traps.** → Prioritize **Compiling and referencing an explicit catalog of recurring cognitive errors to spot and mitigate systematic irrationality** over _Relying on unassisted intuition or striving for unattainable, total cognitive perfection through sheer willpower_ because Human reasoning errors are not random mistakes but systematic deviations from logic that repeat predictably in specific directions; maintaining clear categories and labels enables early recognition and course correction before lasting damage occurs.. (Rule: `Compiling and referencing an explicit catalog of recurring cognitive errors to spot and mitigate systematic irrationality`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Approaching a comprehensive curriculum, audit, or system for error reduction and rational decision-making.** → Prioritize **Surveying the structured landscape of cognitive biases as an interconnected taxonomy** over _Treating reasoning errors as isolated ad-hoc anomalies or diving into individual topics without structural orientation_ because Human cognitive flaws are systematic, recurring, and directional; mapping out the full catalog provides an overarching navigational schema necessary for targeted bias-checking and debiasing.. (Rule: `Surveying the structured landscape of cognitive biases as an interconnected taxonomy`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Evaluating high-risk ventures, investments, career choices, or analyzing purported 'success factors' of winning individuals and companies.** → Prioritize **Actively investigating the unseen graveyard of failures to calibrate realistic base rates of success** over _Judging probabilities and success factors based solely on visible, high-profile winners_ because Triumph is inherently more visible than failure in media and culture, leading to systematic overestimation of success odds and the false attribution of shared survivor traits as unique causal success factors.. (Rule: `Actively investigating the unseen graveyard of failures to calibrate realistic base rates of success`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Evaluating marketing claims, elite institutional credentials, self-help regimens, or aspirational role models promising transformative results.** → Prioritize **Auditing innate selection criteria and pre-existing traits before pursuing an outcome** over _Attributing desirable outcomes to associated training, institutions, products, or self-help habits_ because Desirable traits (such as athletic physiques, elite graduates' success, or chronic happiness) are frequently prerequisites that select candidates into an activity rather than results produced by the activity itself, making efforts to replicate the outcome futile when confounding selection factors are mistaken for causal results.. (Rule: `Auditing innate selection criteria and pre-existing traits before pursuing an outcome`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Evaluating high-noise data streams, streaks, coincidences, or apparent predictive sequences (such as financial markets, random clusters, or sensory noise).** → Prioritize **Treating perceived regularities and predictive formulas in noisy data as pure chance until statistically verified** over _Constructing causal narratives, trading rules, or predictive models based on intuitive pattern recognition_ because The human brain is an oversensitive pattern seeker that routinely invents order, rules, and meaning out of random distributions and diffuse signals.. (Rule: `Treating perceived regularities and predictive formulas in noisy data as pure chance until statistically verified`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Evaluating opinions, beliefs, claims, product superiority, or group actions, especially in ambiguous situations or when under peer or social pressure.** → Prioritize **Independently evaluating intrinsic merits, objective evidence, and logic** over _Defaulting to consensus, popularity, or the behavior of the crowd as a proxy for truth or correctness_ because Social proof is an evolutionary survival heuristic that causes individuals to equate popularity with validity, warping common sense and fueling collective irrationality like market bubbles, panics, and conformity even when consensus is demonstrably foolish.. (Rule: `Independently evaluating intrinsic merits, objective evidence, and logic`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Deciding whether to continue, finance, or terminate an ongoing project, investment, relationship, or activity that is failing to meet objectives.** → Prioritize **Evaluating future costs, benefits, and alternative opportunities from the current moment forward** over _Continuing an underperforming commitment to justify accumulated investments of time, money, or effort_ because Past investments are irrecoverable and unchangeable regardless of whether you persist or exit, whereas staying committed out of an emotional need for consistency or face-saving merely compounds existing losses into a lost cause.. (Rule: `Evaluating future costs, benefits, and alternative opportunities from the current moment forward`) [Tier 2]
