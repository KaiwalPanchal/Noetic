# Framework: Boring Technology

**Source:** Choose Boring Technology — Dan McKinley (https://mcfunley.com/choose-boring-technology)  
**Linked Interests:** [[interests#infrastructure-durability]], [[interests#operational-simplicity]]  
**Tags:** #framework #infrastructure-architecture  
**Admission Score:** 5.0/5.0 (Admission Rubric)  
**The Keyboard Test:** Immediately limits new dependencies to 2 tokens when evaluating stack designs.  

---

## 1. Core Idea
Novelty in production infrastructure creates compounding operational debt; restrict choices to mature, boring solutions.

## 2. Core Heuristic & Trigger
- **When in choosing core operational infrastructure and data stores** → Prioritize **Well-understood, established technologies** over _Novel, cutting-edge tools_ because the operational cost of unknown unknowns far exceeds initial feature benefits. (Rule: `Budget at most two innovation tokens for the entire company stack; reject unbudgeted novelty`) [Tier 2] [Tags: architecture, infrastructure] [Anchor: The appetite of engineering teams for novelty is virtually unlimited, whereas their operational capacity to understand and maintain novel technologies in production is strictly constrained.]
- **When in evaluating technology proposals from team members** → Prioritize **Solving problem with existing installed tech** over _Introducing a new service or database engine_ because each new technology imposes a perpetual operational tax on team cognitive capacity. (Rule: `Require explicit justification of business advantage before approving a new dependency`) [Tier 2] [Tags: governance, infrastructure] [Anchor: Every company or engineering organization starts with a finite, scarce budget: innovation tokens.]
- **When in modeling simple data entities and non-critical table updates** → Prioritize **In-place mutable status updates** over _Append-only transition ledger tables_ because traditional single-row updates minimize initial schema overhead for generic CRUD. (Rule: `Default to single-row updates unless strict audit durability is mandated`) [Tier 2] [Tags: data-architecture] [Anchor: Postgres is boring. Linux is boring. Memcached is boring. When Postgres encounters a deadlock, thousands of engineers have encountered the exact same deadlock]
- **The Core Question / Razor:** "Does this novel technology provide an order-of-magnitude core business advantage worth an innovation token?"
- **Governing Invariant:** Unbudgeted technical novelty directly increases production failure frequency.

## 3. Mental Moves / Procedures
1. Audit the proposal for new operational dependencies.
2. Count the innovation tokens required (max 2 across stack).
3. Verify if existing tools (e.g. Postgres) can solve the problem.
4. Enforce boring defaults unless a clear business multiplier is proven.

## 4. Where to Apply
- **Primary Domain:** infrastructure-architecture
### Ontology Primitives
- **Innovation Token** (`variable`): Scarce organizational budget representing capacity to absorb technical novelty.
- **Boring Technology** (`entity`): Technology whose failure modes and operational characteristics are thoroughly known and documented.
- **Unknown Unknowns** (`force`): Unforeseen production failure states emerging from operational inexperience.

### Trade-Off Matrix
| Tension Dimension | Recommended Choice | Sacrificed | Gained | Boundary Flip Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Technology Selection | Established boring tools (e.g. Postgres) | Trendy developer enthusiasm and bleeding-edge features | Predictable failure modes, massive community answers, battle-tested operational stability | A hard, measured non-functional requirement that Postgres physically cannot satisfy (e.g., sub-millisecond global write consensus under 100k TPS) |

## 5. Anti-Patterns & Boundaries (When it Breaks)
- **Resume-Driven Architecture**: Adopting distributed streaming or graph databases for simple internal CRUD tools → Team spends 80% of bandwidth debugging broker consensus instead of delivering product value (Recovery: Collapse stack to standard relational database and boring web framework)
