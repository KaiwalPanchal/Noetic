## 1. Core Idea
Human social interaction operates as an inescapable, zero-sum game of power modeled after the aristocratic court; survival and ascendancy require mastering perception, concealing intent, deflecting envy, and using indirect manipulation over naive virtue or raw competence.

## 2. Core Heuristic & Trigger
- **When in Navigating hierarchical power dynamics where personal advancement, security, and tenure depend on the goodwill of a superior** → Prioritize **Subordinating personal brilliance and flattering the superior's vanity to keep them preeminently secure** over _Overtly showcasing raw competence, exceptional talent, or independent indispensability to earn merit-based advancement_ because Displaying superior brilliance or virtue triggers acute insecurity and envy in the master, transforming professional admiration into defensive retaliation and downfall. (Rule: `Subordinating personal brilliance and flattering the superior's vanity to keep them preeminently secure`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Initiating high-stakes maneuvers, negotiations, or competitive pursuits where early resistance would defeat the objective** → Prioritize **Concealing true intentions through decoy desires, false indifference, and scrambled signals** over _Directly declaring purpose, emotional transparency, or candid communication of objectives_ because Directly revealing aims allows targets and rivals to erect defenses, anticipate maneuvers, or counter-strategize, whereas ambiguity and misdirection preserve leverage and freedom of movement. (Rule: `Concealing true intentions through decoy desires, false indifference, and scrambled signals`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Engaging in public appearances, formal negotiations, evaluations, or confrontations where perceived status and psychological leverage must be preserved** → Prioritize **Practicing calculated verbal economy, silence, and enigmatic brevity** over _Voluble explanations, extensive self-justification, or attempting to impress through voluminous speech_ because Excessive speech invariably reveals indiscretions, betrays insecurity, and dispels the aura of authority, while brevity forces others to fill the void and expose their hands. (Rule: `Practicing calculated verbal economy, silence, and enigmatic brevity`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Selecting key collaborators, deputies, or operational lieutenants for positions of real authority and execution** → Prioritize **Structuring alliances and appointments around calculated mutual self-interest and former adversaries** over _Elevating personal friends or relying on sentimental bonds, gratitude, and moral reciprocity_ because Friends are blinded by familiarity and latent envy and quickly turn ungrateful or treacherous, whereas former adversaries must constantly prove loyalty and operate on predictable utility. (Rule: `Structuring alliances and appointments around calculated mutual self-interest and former adversaries`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Operating within institutional, civilized, or ostensibly egalitarian environments where overt displays of power and ambition are taboos** → Prioritize **Outward conformity to established courtly decorum, institutional rules, and social niceties paired with covert maneuvers** over _Radical candor, overt challenges to orthodoxies, or attempting to opt out of the political game_ because Direct moral rebellion or displaying overt ambition invites immediate unified resistance and marginalization, whereas adopting the dominant manners disarms suspicion and grants cover for strategic action. (Rule: `Outward conformity to established courtly decorum, institutional rules, and social niceties paired with covert maneuvers`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Establishing long-term positioning and organizational indispensability within unstable or competitive hierarchies** → Prioritize **Cultivating indispensable dependency and controlling critical points of execution or access** over _Teaching subordinates full autonomy, relying on contractually defined goodwill, or assuming past loyalty guarantees safety_ because True security stems from others needing you to achieve their own survival and goals, rather than from their affection or perceived moral obligation. (Rule: `Cultivating indispensable dependency and controlling critical points of execution or access`) [Tier 2]
