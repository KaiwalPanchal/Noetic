## 1. Core Idea
In an era where infotech and biotech merge to challenge human agency, labor utility, and democratic governance while existential ecological and technological threats transcend nation-states, human survival depends on breaking free from bankrupt ideological myths, demystifying internal biochemical algorithms, preventing extreme biological inequality, and cultivating present-focused cognitive clarity to participate in planet-scale governance.

(Rule: `Maintaining cognitive clarity and present-moment focus on urgent macro-disruptions`) [Tier 2]
- **When in When an established sociopolitical or ideological framework fails to predict or manage emerging macro-level disruptions and crises.** → Prioritize **Confronting ideological void and conducting empirical reality checks amid narrative collapse** over _Clinging dogmatically to failing grand narratives or lapsing into disoriented apocalyptic panic_ because Human cognition relies on simplified grand stories to interpret reality, but when historical and technological shifts shatter the dominant narrative, losing that interpretive prism causes elites and societies to either retreat into reactionary illusions or mistake the collapse of their specific story for the literal end of civilization.. (Rule: `Confronting ideological void and conducting empirical reality checks amid narrative collapse`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Evaluating long-term economic trajectories, labor market disruptions, and career/institutional planning in an era of rapid AI and life-sciences advancement.** → Prioritize **Anticipating structural human economic irrelevance driven by the confluence of infotech and biotech** over _Relying on historical precedents of automation and assuming cognitive or emotional tasks remain permanently uniquely human_ because Human intuition and emotional discernment are biochemical algorithms and pattern-recognition processes rather than mystical qualities, meaning AI equipped with biological understanding, connectivity, and updateability can outperform humans across both physical and cognitive domains with no guaranteed third refuge.. (Rule: `Anticipating structural human economic irrelevance driven by the confluence of infotech and biotech`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Evaluating authority, political choices, market decisions, or personal agency in an era where data-processing algorithms can monitor and manipulate human biological mechanisms.** → Prioritize **Interrogating and demystifying internal feelings and desires as hackable biochemical algorithms** over _Sanctifying human feelings, choices, and intuitions as the ultimate, untouchable source of authority and free will_ because Liberal democracy and markets assume feelings reflect sovereign free will, but modern biology and infotech reveal that feelings are biochemical calculations that external Big Data algorithms can increasingly decipher, manipulate, and weaponize, turning reliance on 'the heart' into an emotional puppet show.. (Rule: `Interrogating and demystifying internal feelings and desires as hackable biochemical algorithms`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Technological and economic transitions combine AI-driven mass displacement with proprietary biotechnological and cognitive upgrades.** → Prioritize **Preventing the monopolization of data and technological enhancements to avert permanent biological caste stratification** over _Relying on the twentieth-century assumption that globalization, technology, and welfare-state momentum naturally bend toward egalitarianism_ because When economic value and military power no longer depend on mass human labor, states lose the strategic incentive to invest in the health and education of the masses, allowing economic disparity combined with biotechnology to transform temporary societal gaps into irreversible, biological divides between enhanced superhumans and redundant Homo sapiens.. (Rule: `Preventing the monopolization of data and technological enhancements to avert permanent biological caste stratification`) [Tier 2]
- **When in Evaluating technological solutions, corporate manifestos, or algorithmic platforms designed to remediate modern alienation, loneliness, and social-political fragmentation.** → Prioritize **Anchoring social cohesion in physical, intimate, embodied human communities and demanding structural alignment from platforms** over _Entrusting community-building and social fabric restoration to algorithmic social networks driven by attention-monetization models_ because Humans remain biologically Stone Age animals whose psychological flourishing depends on small-scale, embodied intimacy that abstract imagined networks cannot replicate, especially when corporate business models prioritize harvesting attention over genuine social connection.. (Rule: `Anchoring social cohesion in physical, intimate, embodied human communities and demanding structural alignment from platforms`) [Tier 2]
